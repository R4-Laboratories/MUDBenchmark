# Outlaw paths

Thieves, assassins, dark priests and barbarians are **paths**, not four more
rank guilds. The social layer stays small: the Mages' Guild, the Wardens and
the Night Market. The outlaw identities come from factions, weapons and
quartermaster gear, so a character can change path by changing equipment.

## The Night Market

The Night Market is a rank guild in the Smuggler's Cache (room 29).

| Rank | Requirement | Perk |
| --- | --- | --- |
| runner | join at level 3 | — |
| cutpurse | level 5 | +1 armour |
| shadow | level 8 | +1 weapon damage |
| master | level 10 | +1 armour and +1 weapon damage |

The night broker promotes in the cache. The guild quartermaster sells:

| Item | Slot | Gate | Price |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| a thief's dagger | mainhand, damage 3, Backstab | runner | 35 |
| a night cloak | cloak, armour 2 | cutpurse | 40 |
| night market boots | feet, armour 1 | cutpurse | 45 |
| night market gloves | hands, armour 1 | cutpurse | 40 |
| an assassin's blade | mainhand, damage 5, Backstab | shadow | 80 |

## The Ashen Hand

The Ashen Hand is a faction, not a guild. Its contact is the ashen priest at
the Reliquary (room 58). Standing comes from clearing the things the Hand
considers false:

- choir moth +2
- mirror-wraith +1

Its quartermaster sells an ashen robe (body, armour 2, standing 3), an ashen
hood (head, armour 1), ashen boots (feet, armour 1), and an unholy mace
(mainhand, damage 4, Drain, standing 6).

## The Red Company

The Red Company musters at the Fen Gate (room 71). Its contact is the red
captain. Standing comes from the Eclipse Court:

- star hound +1
- sundial knight +2
- eclipse regent +3

Its quartermaster sells a red company axe (mainhand, damage 6, Rage, standing
3), a red company helm (head, armour 2), red company gauntlets (hands, armour
1), and red company boots (feet, armour 2, standing 6).

## The three path techniques

| Technique | Weapon | Effect | Cooldown |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Backstab | thief's dagger, assassin's blade | +6 damage on the next blow | 9 s |
| Rage | red company axe | +5 damage on the next blow | 9 s |
| Drain | unholy mace | next blow heals half the damage dealt | 9 s |

The setup is spent on the next combat round, hit or miss. The weapon determines
the move; the guild or faction determines what gear is available. No quests
were added.

## Why not four guilds?

Four new rank guilds would mean four halls, rosters, rank ladders and vaults
for very little play. The Night Market gives thieves and assassins a social
home. Dark priests and barbarians get identity through their factions and
quartermasters. Their capabilities come from weapons and the existing technique
system, so the next outlaw can be added as a weapon and a standing track rather
than another institution.
