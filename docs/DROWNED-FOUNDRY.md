# The Drowned Foundry — a second party dungeon

The Drowned Foundry is a **12-room level 9–10 party dungeon** below the
Deepdelve. It is the second site built for the party layer, and it adds a guild
shortcut: a Night Market runner can pick the valve instead of turning it with a
party.

## Getting there

- Reach **The Underground Pool (212)** in the Deepdelve.
- With a party of at least two in the room, `turn wheel`. The drowned stair
  opens `down` to **The Drowned Stair (214)**.
- The stair is level 9. The office and lower foundry are level 10.

## Map

```text
The Underground Pool (212)
          | down* (party wheel)
The Drowned Stair (214) --east--> The Valve Gallery (215) --east--> The Cold Furnace (216) --east--> The Casting Floor (217)
                                      | down* (party valve / Night Market pick)
                               The Flooded Sump (218)
                                      | east* (party sluice)
                               The Crane Room (219) --east--> The Master's Office (220)
                                      | down* (party crane)          | down
                               The Bellows Hall (222) <---------- The Cooling Tunnels (221)
                                      | east* (party bellows)
                               The Slag Bridge (223) --east--> The Foundry Heart (224) --north--> The Master's Forge (225)
```

`*` is a secret exit opened by a room action.

## Party actions and the guild shortcut

| Room | Command | Requirement | Opens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 212 The Underground Pool | `turn wheel` | party of 2 here | `down` to 214 |
| 215 The Valve Gallery | `turn valve` | party of 2 here | `down` to 218 |
| 215 The Valve Gallery | `pick valve` | Night Market runner | `down` to 218 |
| 218 The Flooded Sump | `open sluice` | party of 2 here | `east` to 219 |
| 219 The Crane Room | `turn crane` | party of 2 here | `down` to 222 |
| 222 The Bellows Hall | `pump bellows` | party of 2 here | `east` to 223 |

A party action reveals the way to every member in the room. The valve has two
solutions: a party, or a Night Market runner's tool. The guild shortcut saves
the party a body at the valve but does not bypass the sluice, crane or bellows.

## Light and levels

The drowned stair, valve gallery, sump, crane room, office, cooling tunnels,
bellows hall and slag bridge are **dark**. Two lanterns are in the site (214 and
220); a party should still bring its own. The cold furnace, casting floor,
foundry heart and master's forge are lit by old heat.

The first six rooms are **level 9**; the office, cooling tunnels, bellows hall,
slag bridge, heart and forge are **level 10**.

## The Foundry quest

The **foundry foreman** at 214 gives **The Drowned Foundry** (level 9,
repeatable each two-hour reset):

1. Reach the master's office (220).
2. Recover the foundry ledger (220).
3. Defeat the foundry master (225).
4. Bring the master's core (225) to the foreman (214).

Reward: **420 XP and 70 coins**. The ledger's last word is DROWNED. The core is
also the master's loot.

## Treasure

- **A foundry hammer** (217, damage 6, Bash technique) — the site's weapon.
- **A foundry apron** (220, body armour 3).
- **A dark ingot** (217, value 60) and **a heap of cold coins** (221, 40 coins).
- **The foundry ledger** (220, readable, value 40) — the quest object.
- **The foundry master's core** (225, value 100) — the quest object.

## The master

The foundry master is level 10 with **120 HP, 7 damage and 5 armour** in
production. A level-10 pair with the foundry hammer and a sword wins with about
23 HP each; a solo character is likely to lose a level. The master's kill is
shared with the party (50% XP and quest kill credit to members in the room).

## Why it matters

The Deepdelve proved a party can open a way. The Foundry adds a second,
harder site with a **guild shortcut**, a **level-10 boss**, a **repeatable
quest** and a party route that cannot be soloed. It also gives the Night Market
a non-combat use for its rank outside the hide skill.
