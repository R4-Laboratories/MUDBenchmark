# The Merry Dancer — a sunken wreck

The Merry Dancer is a **12-room level 7–9 wreck** off the Harbor, reached by a
salvage skiff from the Docks. It adds a boat route, a party capstan, a keyed
strongbox and a drowned captain.

## Getting there

- At **The Docks (31)**, `board skiff` to **The Wreck Buoy (240)**. The skiff is
  free; the red buoy is a short row out.
- The return is the same: `board skiff` at 240, or walk `north` to the docks.
- The first six rooms are **level 7**; the galley through the shrine are
  **level 8**; the captain's berth and cabin are **level 9**.

`*` is the secret cargo hatch opened by the party capstan.

## Light and levels

Every room below the buoy is **dark**. The buoy has a wreck lantern and the
cargo hold has a second; bring one each. The capstan at 241 needs a party of
two and opens the hatch down to the cargo hold; the long way is 242 south.

## Key, strongbox and quest

The **strongbox key** is in the flooded galley (246). It opens the **great iron
strongbox** in the strongroom (244), which holds the **tide-glass**. The
**captain's log** is in the captain's cabin (243).

The **salvager** at 240 gives **The Merry Dancer's Glass** (level 7, repeatable
each two-hour reset):

1. Reach the captain's cabin (243).
2. Recover the captain's log (243).
3. Defeat the drowned captain (251).
4. Bring the tide-glass (244) to the salvager (240).

Reward: **300 XP and 45 coins**. The log's last entry says the glass should not
be opened in the hold.

## The captain and the treasure

- **The drowned captain** (251) is level 9 with **90 HP, 6 damage and 3 armour**
  in production. A level-9 pair with the foundry hammer and a sword wins with
  about 21 HP each; the kill is shared with the party.
- **A handful of drowned coins** (247, 45 coins).
- **Two wreck lanterns** (240, 245) and **the captain's log** (243, readable,
  value 35).
- **The tide-glass** (244, value 70) is the quest object.

## Why it matters

The wreck is the boat site the Harbor was missing: a free skiff, a party
capstan, a keyed strongbox, a dark hull and a repeatable salvage contract. It
also adds two hidden ways, bringing completion to **25 quests and 25 hidden
ways**.
