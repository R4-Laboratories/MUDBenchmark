# The Flooded Monastery — a dark story site

The Flooded Monastery is a **14-room level 8–10 site** below the Grey Keep
ossuary. It is built around a keyed chest, a party bell rope, a dark crypt and a
drowned abbot.

## Getting there

- From **The Ossuary (147)** in the Grey Keep, go `down` to **The Flooded Porch
  (226)**.
- The porch and the first seven rooms are **level 8**; the scriptorium through
  the abbot's cell are **level 9**; the chapel and reliquary are **level 10**.
- Bring a light. Most of the monastery is dark; there are two lanterns inside.

237; 238 north is 239.


`*` is a secret exit opened by the party bell.

## Light and levels

The porch, refectory, kitchen, cell corridor, bell stair, flooded cell,
scriptorium, sacristy, crypt and abbot's cell are **dark**. The cloister, bell
tower, chapel and reliquary are open or candlelit. A lantern is at 226 and a
second at 233.

## The key and the clapper

- **A small iron key** is in the kitchen (229). It unlocks the **banded chest**
  in the sacristy (234) with `unlock chest`.
- The chest holds **the abbey clapper**, the quest object.
- The **bell rope** at 235 needs a party of two and the clapper in hand:
  `pull bell` reveals the secret `south` stair to **The Abbot's Cell (237)**.
  The crypt route (234 down to 236, east to 237) is the long way.

## The Bell Below quest

The **bellkeeper** at 226 gives **The Bell Below** (level 8, repeatable each
two-hour reset):

1. Climb the bell tower (235).
2. Recover the monastery ledger (233).
3. Defeat the drowned abbot (238).
4. Bring the abbey clapper to the bellkeeper (226).

Reward: **380 XP and 60 coins**. The ledger records the abbey's last year; the
last page says DROWNED.

## Treasure and the abbot

- **A silver bell relic** (239, neck armour 2, value 90) is behind the abbot.
- **A handful of old coins** (229, 30 coins) and **the monastery ledger** (233,
  readable, value 25).
- The **drowned abbot** (238) is level 10 with **110 HP, 6 damage and 4 armour**
  in production. A level-10 pair with the foundry hammer and a sword wins with
  about 38 HP each. The kill is shared with the party.

## Why it matters

The monastery is the story site the Grey Keep was missing: a key, a party bell,
a dark crypt, a repeatable quest and a boss behind a real route. It also adds a
guard in the quest table: a quest cannot both collect and deliver the same
object, because delivery consumes it and the collect fact can never be true at
completion.
